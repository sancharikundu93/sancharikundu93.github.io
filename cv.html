Research Experience

2023 – present
Ph.D. Graduate Researcher, School of Plant and Environmental Sciences, Virginia Tech (Blacksburg, VA, USA).
  • Cell-type-specific transcriptional responses to salt stress in pennycress roots: snRNA-seq under salinity resolving cell-type responses and alternative splicing / polyadenylation isoform diversity; bulk RNA-seq temporal deconvolution; confocal and brightfield root imaging; growth-chamber experiments.
  • Refining 3′ UTR annotation to improve scRNA-seq quantification in ten non-model plants.
  • Cross-species spatial annotation for pepper (Stereo-seq) using pathway-specific enrichment to identify cell types and tissue domains.
  • OMG 2.0: scaling the Orthogroup Marker Gene annotation method and building a web resource with organ- and condition-specific marker features.
  • Spatial maps from single-cell data: confocal imaging of Arabidopsis fluorescent marker lines to build a 2D root model and map scRNA-seq data.
Research keywords: single-nucleus RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, salt stress, cross-species annotation, pennycress (Thlaspi arvense), pepper (Capsicum), Arabidopsis thaliana.
2018 – 2022
Graduate Researcher, Department of Biological Sciences, University of New Hampshire (Durham, NH, USA).
  • Role of putrescine and spermidine in salt and cadmium tolerance in hybrid poplar (clone NM6): gas exchange (LI-COR 6400) and HPLC quantification of sugars, polyamines, phytochelatins and amino acids.
Research keywords: polyamines, abiotic stress, plant physiology, hybrid poplar (Populus).
