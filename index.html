Plant Single-Cell Biologist & Computational Genomics

Sanchari Kundu

I study how individual plant cells respond to environmental stress. Working at the interface of wet lab and dry lab, I combine single-nucleus and spatial transcriptomics, imaging and cross-species annotation to link gene function to stress resilience, with the goal of building more resilient food crops.

Reading how plants respond to stress, one cell type at a time.
